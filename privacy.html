Privacy policy

Home dashboard is a private, self-hosted application used by one household. It is not offered to the public.

What data is accessed

When a family member connects their Google account, the dashboard accesses their Google Calendar (the list of calendars and their events) through the Google Calendar API.

How the data is used

Calendar events are used only to show them on the household dashboard and to keep the dashboard calendar and the linked Google calendars in sync. Events created or changed on the dashboard are written back to the linked Google calendar.

Where the data is stored

Events and the sign-in tokens are stored only on the household's own server at home. They are not sent to the developer or to any other third party, are not sold, and are not used for advertising or analytics.

Voice and typed commands are sent to Google's Gemini API to understand them. Calendar data from Google is not included in those requests.

Removing access

Syncing of a calendar can be stopped at any time in the dashboard settings. Access to the Google account can be removed at any time under Third-party apps & services in the Google account; this stops all syncing. Events already shown on the dashboard can be deleted there.

Use of Google user data

Home dashboard's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
